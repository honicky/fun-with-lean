## Built for translational scale

When you're comparing responders to non-responders across dozens of samples,
the last thing you want is annotation drift between batches. MiraTyper applies
one universal classifier to every dataset, so cell-type composition is
comparable cohort to cohort.

- **Target discovery** — find the populations that actually differ between arms.
- **Pathology & spatial** — type cells in tissue context, not just suspensions.
- **Biomarker discovery** — composition shifts you can trust across studies.

Request a demo and we'll run it on a representative slice of your data.
