## What you'll learn

- Why reference-based annotation breaks down at atlas scale, and how a single
  universal classifier sidesteps tissue-specific model selection.
- Live walkthrough: annotating a mixed-tissue dataset end-to-end, including
  spatial data from CosMx and Visium.
- How to read **hierarchical F1** instead of flat accuracy so rare populations
  don't disappear into the majority class.
- Where MiraTyper fits into an existing Seurat / Scanpy pipeline.

## Who it's for

Computational biologists, bioinformatics leads, and translational teams who
annotate single-cell or spatial transcriptomics data and are tired of
batch effects and inconsistent calls between studies.

> Bring a dataset. We'll show you what consistent, batch-resistant typing looks
> like on your own data during the follow-up.
